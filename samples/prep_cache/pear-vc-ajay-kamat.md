# Pear VC: Venture Fellow interview prep

## The 60-second version
- Pear is an early-stage firm that invests at pre-seed and seed across AI, B2B, biotech, consumer, deep tech, fintech, and healthcare [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992).
- You're meeting Ajay Kamat. He's a former founder who helps run PearX and invests in consumer, fintech and healthcare.
- Your best angle: you build and judge AI agent products every day, so you can tell a real AI product from a demo.

## The company right now
- In October 2026 Pear closed its $333M Fund V, which brings it to more than $1.1 billion [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992) under management. The fund is about 23% smaller than the $432M Pear raised in May 2023. [[2]](https://techfundingnews.com/doordash-vanta-backer-pear-vc-closes-333m-seed-fund-to-back-the-next-generation-of-ai-startups/)
- Most of the fund goes to new pre-seed and seed deals deployed over roughly two and a half years [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992).
- PearX runs twice a year and accepts fewer than 1% of applicants. More than 6,000 startups applied to the Summer 2026 cohort alone. [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992)
- Recent wins: Vals AI (PearX S23), an independent evaluator of AI models, raised $40 million led by Andreessen Horowitz. [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992) Phinity Labs (PearX S25) reached eight figures in annualized run rate. [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992)

## Your interviewer(s)
**Ajay Kamat**
- When Fund V closed in October 2026, Pear promoted Arash Afrakhteh, Ajay Kamat and Shravan Reddy to general partner; Kamat invests in consumer, fintech and healthcare [[1]](https://www.webull.com/news/15745802813268992) [[2]](https://techfundingnews.com/doordash-vanta-backer-pear-vc-closes-333m-seed-fund-to-back-the-next-generation-of-ai-startups/).
- Before Pear he co-founded and was CEO of Wedding Party, which he ran for about five years before it was acquired by Instacart [[3]](https://pear.vc/members/ajay-kamat/). He joined Pear in 2015 [[3]](https://pear.vc/members/ajay-kamat/). He has a B.S. in Biological Systems Engineering from UC Davis [[3]](https://pear.vc/members/ajay-kamat/).
- He helps run PearX and works closely with portfolio founders [[3]](https://pear.vc/members/ajay-kamat/) as they look for product-market fit.
- His first startup was MicroMobs, a group messaging service he later shut down. In a 2017 FundersClub profile he said he wished the team had pivoted and shut the product down earlier [[4]](https://fundersclub.com/blog/2017/05/11/founders-should-be-skeptical-their-own-products-early-pear-vcs-ajay-kamat/).

What he likely cares about: founder speed, being honest about traction, and spotting founders early.

## What they will probably ask
1. **Why venture?** Talk about how you see early AI startups from the inside at Quest Space: 42 PRs in about six weeks and shipping fixes before a launch deadline.
2. **How would you judge an AI startup?** Use your Algoverse LLM-bias research and the Storefront audit harness (contract gate plus LLM judge). The point: test whether outputs can be verified, not how good the demo looks.
3. **Pitch a company or trend.** Connect it to Vals AI and AI evaluation, an area you have hands-on experience with.
4. **How would you source student founders?** Use your Berkeley network and the SCET workflow role, which puts you close to student founders.

## Questions to ask them
- Fund V is smaller than Fund IV and is planned to be deployed over about two and a half years. How does that change what fellows should bring in?
- With 6,000+ PearX applicants, what separates the top 1% early on?
- Now that you're a GP, what are you most excited about in consumer AI?

## Watch-outs
- The recruiter's email calls him "Partner," and Pear's own bio page still says Partner, but the Fund V announcement in October 2026 says he was promoted to general partner. If his title comes up, congratulate him on the promotion.
- Your resume is heavy on engineering. Be ready to talk about markets and founders too, not just code.
