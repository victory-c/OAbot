# Precursor Ventures: Venture Fellow interview prep

## The 60-second version
- **The firm:** Precursor is a pre-seed and seed stage venture capital firm based in San Francisco. They invest in people over product [[1]](https://precursorvc.com/llms.txt).
- **Your interviewer:** Ashtan Jordan, Principal. A former founder who began in investment banking and now invests at Precursor.
- **Your best angle:** You build and evaluate AI agents hands-on (Quest Space, Storefront audit harness). That lets you check AI startups' claims technically. Your Algoverse bias research also fits a firm that avoids screening founders by credentials.

## The company right now
- Precursor co-led (with Neo) the pre-seed round of Courtroom, which builds AI jury and judge simulation for litigation teams, announced in June 2026 [[2]](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260611125975/en/).
- Precursor led Xella Health's pre-seed round of more than $3.7M for a women's precision health platform, announced in January 2026 [[3]](https://hlth.com/insights/news/xella-health-raises-3-7-million-pre-seed-to-launch-women-s-precision-health-platform-2026-01-14).
- Precursor took part in Claryx's $3.5M pre-seed round, led by Outlander VC, for genomic surveillance of hospital-acquired infections (August 2026) [[4]](https://pulse2.com/claryx-raises-3-5-million-pre-seed-led-by-outlander-vc/).
- In April 2025, Precursor Ventures announced the close of its fifth fund, Precursor Ventures Fund V, with $66M. [[5]](https://www.everythingstartups.com/vc-funds/precursor-ventures)
- One profile says the firm deliberately avoids the algorithmic screening for elite schools and employers that bigger funds use [[6]](https://www.vcsheet.com/fund/precursor-ventures).

## Your interviewer(s)
**Ashtan Jordan, Principal**
- The Kauffman Fellows profile says Ashtan leads investments across B2B software, healthcare, and consumer technology [[7]](https://www.kauffmanfellows.org/fellows/ashtan-jordan). Precursor's own site lists a focus on AI systems and applications and advanced industry [[1]](https://precursorvc.com/llms.txt).
- Before Precursor, Ashtan founded The MVX Group, a product management staffing agency, and was on the founding team of SAMPLEit, a beauty discovery platform [[7]](https://www.kauffmanfellows.org/fellows/ashtan-jordan).
- Ashtan began as an investment banking analyst at Citigroup [[7]](https://www.kauffmanfellows.org/fellows/ashtan-jordan).
- Education: Howard University and the Stanford Graduate School of Business. Ashtan is a Kauffman Fellow [[7]](https://www.kauffmanfellows.org/fellows/ashtan-jordan).
- In the Xella Health announcement, Ashtan praised the team's rare combination of scientific depth, product intuition, and empathy [[3]](https://hlth.com/insights/news/xella-health-raises-3-7-million-pre-seed-to-launch-women-s-precision-health-platform-2026-01-14).
- **What Ashtan likely cares about:** how good the founders are and how strong the product is.

## What they will probably ask
1. **Why VC, and why pre-seed?** Say that building at Quest Space showed you how much early technical choices matter, for example the agent-harness SDK skeleton that the team adopted.
2. **How would you judge an AI startup?** Use the Storefront harness: check whether claims are backed by evidence, using a contract gate plus an LLM judge. Then ask the same of their demo.
3. **Pitch a company or space you like.** Point to the agent-reliability problems you fixed (file grounding, source verification) as an opening for startups.
4. **Tell us about research.** Algoverse: you tested LLM resume screening with name substitution. Tie this to fair founder evaluation.

## Questions to ask them
- Precursor backed both Xella Health and Claryx. How do you weigh technical depth against founder-market fit in health?
- How do you tell real AI agents from thin wrappers at pre-seed?
- What does a firm that invests in people over product look for in a first meeting?

## Watch-outs
- The role scope wasn't public, so ask what fellows actually do.
- Don't overclaim investing experience. Your strength is technical diligence.
